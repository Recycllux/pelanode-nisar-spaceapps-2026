# RiverNexa

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, challenge "Dancing with the SARs". Team Recycllux, Bucharest.

RiverNexa uses NISAR L-band radar to show where the Lower Danube lost water during the 2026 drought, including water hidden under reeds that optical satellites cannot see. It links that loss to river levels and proposes where field probes should go next. It is designed for the Danube Delta Biosphere Reserve Administration (ARBDD) as an early-warning indicator: correlation, not prediction of cause.

**App:** `docs/` (GitHub Pages). Every layer is labelled **Real** (measured), **Simulated** (illustration of a workflow) or **Future** (model output).

## What we found (11 NISAR dates, 18 Jun – 28 Oct 2026)

- **Danube Delta:** water under reeds and floodplain forest fell from about 838 km² (18 Jun) to about 104 km² (16 Oct). Open lakes and channels stayed near 1,000 km². The loss was almost entirely hidden under vegetation.
- **River:** all seven Romanian gauges from Cernavodă to Tulcea fell below their official low-water marks in early July 2026 and stayed there. Isaccea reached −1 cm on 19 Oct; its 2015–2025 minimum was 23 cm.
- **Link:** Delta water under vegetation follows the Isaccea level from about 6 days earlier best (Spearman ρ ≈ 0.93 over 11 dates).
- **Cernavodă:** small but visible sandbank exposure in the channel between 24 Jul and 17 Aug.
- **Sentinel-1 check:** NISAR and Sentinel-1 open water in the Delta overlap well (median IoU ≈ 0.74 on 7 near-simultaneous dates). Over ten years, 2026 has the lowest typical Delta open water (837 km² vs 874–891 km² in 2016–2024), but only by about 5%: the drought shows mainly under the reeds.
- **Prediction:** trained on ten years of Sentinel-1, TabPFN-3.5 predicts Delta open water 6–12 days ahead with about 20 km² area error in 2025 and 2026, vs 31–33 km² for "no change".
- **Balta Mică a Brăilei:** little change. The zone is mostly dyked farmland, which makes it a useful control.

Numbers are regenerated by the pipeline; the app reads them from `docs/data/`.

## Method

| Step | Script | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | `pipeline/a1_search_nisar.py` | Finds NISAR L2 GCOV granules (track 065 descending, frames 065 + 066) in NASA CMR. |
| A2 | `pipeline/a2_fetch_nisar.py`, `remote_h5.py` | Reads only our three zones from each HDF5 file with HTTP range requests (no full download). HH and HV at 20 m, UTM 35N. |
| B1–B3 | `b1_fetch_river.py`, `b2_fetch_glofas.py`, `b3_glofas_stations.py` | DanubeAlert daily levels and official thresholds 2015–2026; GloFAS v4 discharge (modelled). |
| H1 | `h1_river_table.py` | Daily drought stage per gauge against the 2015–2025 climatology and the low-water mark. |
| F0 | `f0_ancillary.py` | ESA WorldCover, Copernicus DEM, GLO-30 HAND and JRC Global Surface Water on the NISAR grid. |
| F1 | `f1_water.py` | Quegan multitemporal speckle filter. Open water: per-date reference to permanent water, inside the area JRC has ever mapped as water (dry fields are as dark as water at the noise floor). Water under vegetation: L-band double bounce (bright HH, HH/HV > 5 dB) in reeds and floodplain forest below 5 m HAND. |
| G0 | `g0_sam_refine.py` | SAM 2.1 (hiera base+) refines open-water shapes from box and point prompts; a mask is kept only if it agrees with the physics seed (IoU ≥ 0.5). |
| G1 | `g1_products.py` | Web layers, lost-water layer, radar colour composite and the area table. |
| K1 | `k1_change_probes.py` | 500 m change cells, per-pixel flooding-threshold map, lag analysis, proposed probe sites. |
| S1–S4 | `s1_fetch.py`, `s2_water.py`, `s3_analysis.py`, `s4_tabpfn_history.py` | Sentinel-1 from NASA OPERA RTC-S1 (C-band, 30 m, track 036): every 6 days in 2026 and about every 12 days Jun–Nov 2016–2025. Open water only (C-band barely enters reeds). Used to check the NISAR maps, fill NISAR's 24 Jul–17 Aug gap, rank 2026 against ten years, and train TabPFN-3.5 on 2016–2024. Windy dates are flagged and left out. |
| K2 | `k2_tabpfn.py` | TabPFN-3.5 (Prior Labs API) predicts water under vegetation 12 days ahead. Time-blocked test on the last five dates against persistence and gradient boosting. |

Run with Python 3.13: `pip install -r requirements.txt`, then the scripts in order (G0 uses `requirements-sam.txt`, and K2 and S4 use `requirements-tabpfn.txt`, each in its own environment, because tabpfn-client needs an older pandas). Tests: `pytest tests`.

## Limits

- Dates before 28 Oct are NISAR **Provisional** products. There is no acquisition on this track between 24 Jul and 17 Aug.
- Reeds also change through the summer, which dims HH; wind can brighten open water. The river link is a correlation over 11 dates.
- 20 m pixels miss narrow features. GloFAS does not split the Delta branches, so Tulcea and Sulina show total Delta inflow.
- The TabPFN prediction beats "no change" only slightly in a flat drought period; it is shown as Future, not as a forecast to act on.
- Probe sites are Simulated: no probe network exists yet.
